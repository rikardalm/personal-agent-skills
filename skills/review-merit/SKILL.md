---
name: review-merit
description: Run when given one or more reviews or feedback items to judge what is valid, prioritize the high-merit points, and act on them with sound judgment.
---

## Steps
1. Parse each review or feedback item and restate the core concern.
2. Evaluate merit by checking correctness, impact, scope, and alignment with project goals.
3. Prioritize high-merit items and decide the best action for each (implement, adjust, or decline with rationale).
4. Apply changes using best judgment and avoid overfitting to low-signal feedback.
5. Summarize what was changed, what was not, and why.
