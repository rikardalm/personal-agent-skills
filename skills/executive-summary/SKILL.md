---
name: executive-summary
description: Run when asked to turn notes, reports, reviews, plans, research, incidents, project state, or long discussions into a concise decision-ready executive summary.
---

## Steps
1. Identify the audience, decision context, and source material scope.
2. Extract the highest-signal facts: goal, current state, impact, risks, decisions, and next actions.
3. Separate confirmed facts from assumptions, interpretations, and open questions.
4. Prioritize what an executive needs to know now, removing detail that does not affect the decision.
5. Present a concise summary with recommendations, next steps, owners, and caveats where available.
